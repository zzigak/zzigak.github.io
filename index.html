Žiga Kovačič

Pronounced Zhee-guh Koh-VAH-chich. It's Slovenian.

I'm an incoming CS PhD student at Stanford, advised by Jiajun Wu. I'm interested in hierarchical world models that learn the right level of physical abstraction for each task and co-evolve through interaction. I also have a deep interest in physical simulation.

Previously, I completed my undergrad at Cornell, where I was fortunate to work with Kevin Ellis and was recognised as a Merrill Presidential Scholar.
